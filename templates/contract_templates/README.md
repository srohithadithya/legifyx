# Standard Contract Templates for Indian SMEs

This directory contains SME-friendly contract templates that have been reviewed for common business scenarios.

## Available Templates

### 1. Employment Agreement
A balanced employment contract suitable for small and medium businesses.

### 2. Service Agreement
Standard service contract for engaging consultants and service providers.

### 3. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
Mutual NDA for protecting confidential information in business dealings.

### 4. Vendor Agreement
Standard vendor/supplier contract with balanced terms.

### 5. Lease Agreement
Commercial lease agreement template for office/shop spaces.

## Usage

These templates are provided as starting points. Always consult with a legal professional before finalizing any contract.

## Key Features of Our Templates

- ✅ Balanced terms protecting both parties
- ✅ Clear and plain language
- ✅ Compliance with Indian laws
- ✅ Reasonable liability caps
- ✅ Fair termination clauses
- ✅ Dispute resolution provisions
